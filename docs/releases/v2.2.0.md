### 🎨 New projects

- **[Orario](https://github.com/stefanutz02/orario)** is a school timetable generator for Romanian schools. Its own solver combines MRV backtracking, ejection chains and simulated annealing on an effort curve. It never schedules a teacher outside their availability, explains why a slot is blocked, and suggests swaps between classes. Timetables export to PDF. Built with React, TypeScript, Node.js and MySQL. Live at [orario.stefanvasilescu.com](https://orario.stefanvasilescu.com)
- **[Arhiva Note](https://github.com/stefanutz02/arhiva-note)** is a local-first app where students and parents track grades, absences, averages, the timetable, homework and projects. It shows the risk of being called on in each subject, simulates future grades and generates five PDF reports. There's no account and no cloud: all data stays in one SQLite file. Built with React, TypeScript, Express and SQLite
